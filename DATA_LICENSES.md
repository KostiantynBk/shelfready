The code in this repository is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`). The data used by the demo comes from these sources:

| Source | Used for | Licence | Attribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Amazon Berkeley Objects (ABO)](https://amazon-berkeley-objects.s3.amazonaws.com/index.html) | Real product photos and listing metadata (catalogue health audit, supplier photo sets) | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | Collins et al., "ABO: Dataset and Benchmarks for Real-World 3D Object Understanding", CVPR 2022. Photos are used unmodified. |
| [Shopping Queries Dataset (ESCI)](https://github.com/amazon-science/esci-data) | Product titles and descriptions for audit text (if used) | Apache-2.0 | Reddy et al., 2022 |
| [Shopify Product Taxonomy](https://github.com/Shopify/product-taxonomy) | Category paths and attribute names | MIT | © Shopify Inc. |

**Synthetic data:** supplier spec sheets, care labels, drawings and supplier emails for the fictional shop "Casa Nord" are generated by `data/scripts/` and released under MIT. Planted defects and their ground truth are documented in `data/defects.yaml`.

Products, suppliers and companies in the synthetic data are fictional.
