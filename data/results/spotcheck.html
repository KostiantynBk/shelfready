Photo spot check

Each question is about what you can see in the photos, compared with what the listing says (category and colour name, shown next to the photos). Click a photo to open it full size. Answer Yes if the statement is true, No if it is not, Not sure if you cannot tell. Sizes, fibre content and empty fields are checked from the listing text by code, so they are not asked here. Answers are kept in this browser; when you are done, click Download answers.
